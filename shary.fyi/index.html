My Bio

A cyborgian entity obsessed with the nature of being, Sharyfairy is an experimental artist and butoh practitioner from Singapore. Their transdisciplinary practice is centred on exploration of the human body as immaculate technology. With energy as their medium and mystic frameworks as their guide, they seek to define a futurist esoteric that tends to the divine.
